Study Sprint is a student study-planning, practice and focus tool. It is not an official examination, school or government service. You remain responsible for checking syllabus details, question answers, exam dates and your own study decisions. Scores and leaderboard rankings are personal practice indicators, not verified examination results or promises of admission.

## Responsible use

Use the app for lawful learning. Do not use it to cheat in examinations, harass others, impersonate a student, manipulate rankings or upload information you do not have permission to share. Only import or distribute questions, documents and images you own or are permitted to use. Your original content remains yours.

## Optional accounts and public sharing

Google sign-in and public leaderboard sharing are separate optional choices. Before permitting public sharing, read the Privacy Policy and understand that your app profile name, photo and scores will be visible to other leaderboard users. No Google account or moderator approval is required after you give permission. You may withdraw sharing through Hide my profile. Review the policy for offline removal and retention limits.

You choose whether to grant Android permissions. App limits depend on those permissions and Android behavior. System settings, calling apps, browser versions of services and separate profiles may fall outside protection. Do not rely on the blocker, reminders or timer as a safety-critical service. Turning off permissions can stop their operation.

## Students and guardians

If you are under 18, involve a parent or guardian before using optional online features. Obtain any permission required where you live before publishing personal information. The app does not verify age or guardian approval. Local study features remain available without public sharing.

## Optional AI study help

Study buddy uses an app-owner managed Firebase service with OpenRouter/NVIDIA tutor models, optional Mistral text backup and Tavily exam-source search and NVIDIA image understanding. Online MCQs are restricted to the chosen MHT-CET, JEE or NEET exam; matching study sources may be used to create original AI practice questions when complete sourced questions are unavailable; no past-paper claim is made for generated questions. AI answers may be inaccurate; verify them against your study material. Attach no more than four photos per question and only content you are permitted to share. Service availability, provider terms and daily allowances apply. NVIDIA access may be subject to trial limits; the app does not promise unlimited free access. Never publish API keys or private content in the public bug-report tracker.
